ABOUT JAIL OPERATIONS

The Forrest County Correctional Center is a medium security facility with a footprint that encompasses 184,000 square feet, including the administrative wing, nine offender housing units for pre-trial and sentenced offenders, various support spaces, and programming areas.

The facility is unique in that it is located between two sides of an interstate highway, I-95. The correctional center opened in 1992 and provides for the supervision and security of up to 500 offenders in its care and custody.

The facility operates under a Direct Supervision Management style. Under direct supervision, individuals are classified according to the type of crime they committed and their criminal background. They are housed with similarly classified individuals in units within the jail where officers interact with offenders. The average sentence served is approximately 3 months. The sheriff recognizes that virtually every offender will return to the community, so the sheriff’s office has developed a Reentry Program to prepare them for a successful transition to a law-abiding lifestyle upon their release, so they don’t just fall back into bad habits when they return to their communities. The office provides a wide range of programs and services appropriate to the needs of offenders, which include, but are not limited to: religious services, mentoring programs, individual and group counseling, health education, recreation and leisure time activities, and evidence-based programming. Offenders are also eligible to participate in the Residential Substance Use Treatment Program or the Pre-Trial Substance Use Treatment Program, which focuses on substance use while utilizing a cognitive behavioral therapy approach and evidence-based practices.
