We seek to employ talented individuals committed to joining a professional organization dedicated to public safety in Forrest County. In support of the Sheriff's mission to serve the citizens of Forrest County by enhancing public safety, employees are tasked with operating a safe, secure, and humane correctional facility through direct supervision. To accomplish this goal, new employees become part of a highly trained, dedicated, professional, compassionate, and diverse workforce.

The level of responsibility of our employees requires that we take great care in recruiting the best candidates we can find. The Forrest Sheriff's Office is committed to identifying hard-working and dedicated individuals pursuing careers in public safety/law enforcement. Our organization seeks individuals committed to the values of honor, integrity, and selfless service.
