Public Safety Division

Special Operations

The Forrest Sheriff’s Office has built strong partnerships with local police departments and public safety agencies. Working at the invitation of local police chiefs the Forrest Sheriff’s Office provides support to local law enforcement. Over half of our uniformed staff have completed training through the Municipal Police Training Committee Reserve Intermittent Academy and are sworn as Deputy Sheriffs. Forrest Deputy Sheriffs are available assist local police departments with large events, searches for missing persons, and K9 searches when requested. Deputies also work in collaboration with law enforcement partners as part of the Metropolitan Law Enforcement Council (Metro-LEC), South Shore Street Crimes Task Force, the South Shore Drug Task Force, FBI OCDETF Strike Force, and the DEA Tactical Diversion Squad.

Command Center

In 2008 the Forrest Sheriff’s Office received a grant to purchase a mobile command center which can be dispatched to local cities and towns during a crisis, law enforcement operation, rescue operations, or to assist with monitoring large community events. The command center has been utilized frequently to assist other law enforcement agencies throughout Forrest County.

MetroLEC

The Forrest Sheriff's Office realizes that public safety reaches beyond the walls of the jail, and has joined forces with the Metropolitan Law Enforcement Council (MetroLEC). The agency provides 43 southeastern Massachusetts towns with highly specialized law enforcement services such as K9 teams, a computer crime unit, special weapons and tactics (SWAT) team members, and a crisis negotiating team.

Sheriff's Response Team (SRT)

The Sheriff’s Response Team (SRT) is a rapid-response unit of specially trained correction officers who respond to incidents within the correctional center including: housing module disruptions or group demonstrations within a facility or grounds, cell extractions, hostage rescues, high profile offender transportation, warrant apprehensions, search-and-rescue of individuals in the community. They also assist with high-risk situations in the community and unruly offenders in courtroom lockup areas.

Mobile Operations Unit

Forrest County Sheriff’s Office Mobile Operations Unit (MOP) consists of five officers all of whom have completed a Basic Motorcycle Operators Course. The MOP unit has been involved with several significant events over the years dating back to the Democratic National Convention held in the city of Boston in 2004. MOP also assists with high profile security transportation runs.
