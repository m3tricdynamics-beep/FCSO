Public Safety & Operations

Jail Visitation Hours

On-site video visitation is available Tuesday through Sunday from 8:00 AM to 9:00 PM. Visitors must register online at least 24 hours prior to their intended visit.

Civil Process Services

The Civil Division is responsible for the service of court documents including summonses, complaints, subpoenas, and writs of execution. Fees apply for service.
